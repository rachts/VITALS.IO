Methodology

The Math Behind the Metrics

We don't estimate. We calculate. Here's exactly how VITALS.IO evaluates your business.

01 — Fundamentals

Everything starts with the fundamentals.

Customer Acquisition Cost
CAC = (Ad Spend + Sales Cost) / New Customers
Lifetime Value
LTV = (ARPA × Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate
Efficiency Ratios
LTV:CAC Ratio = LTV / CAC
Payback Period = CAC / (ARPA × Gross Margin %)
02 — Composite

The Vitals Health Score

A proprietary weighted index designed to gauge the immediate durability of revenue streams.

LTV:CAC Efficiency 50% WGT

Primary driver of long-term unit viability.

Payback Velocity 30% WGT

Capital liquidity and reinvestment cycle speed.

Gross Margin 10% WGT

Cost of goods sold relative to top-line.

Net Churn 10% WGT

Retention decay across the customer base.

Final Composite
Health Score = Σ (Metric × Weight)
